Terms　BnB=the everyday combo (bread and butter — learn this one first)　|　T!=tornado: spins an airborne opponent to extend the combo, once per combo　|　CH=counter hit: catching an opponent mid-move; most moves gain a launch or crumple on CH　|　Punishers=a guaranteed punish taken from the recovery of a blocked move　|　Staples=a higher-damage route for one particular starter　|　Mix-up=forcing a guess between standing (blocks mid) and crouching (blocks low); the core mix-up is the character's main guessing point
Game plan　Jun Kazama is built around Parries & reversals: control the range in neutral, bait with backdashes, and punish impatience with counter hits. Her Kazama ki moves (the f+2 follow-ups, d+1+2) cost her own health when blocked (partly recoverable); in Heat the cost halves and the chip damage rises, and 1,2,2 and d/b+1,1,1+2 restore health on hit. Poke at close range with b+2 / d+4 / d/b+3, deny at mid range with d/f+4 and b+3, and punish whiffs with f+2 (Onikiri).

Practical tips

TipsNotes
Health managementWhen low on health, punish with 1,2,2 and d/b+1,1,1+2 to heal, and lay off the expensive ki moves
The Izumo mix-upIZU 4,1 (low) against IZU 2 (mid, punishable on block) is the core guess
Use MIA 2 sparinglyMIA 2 is punishable on block, rage arts included — save it for Heat or for the kill
Combo notesCore transitions: f+3,2~IZU and f,F+2~B into MIA; finish with GEN 2 for damage or f,F+2,3 to carry
Against turtlesJun struggles to open up a blocker: use GEN 1 (+3) and the Izumo sweep to make a gap, and wait patiently for a counter hit
