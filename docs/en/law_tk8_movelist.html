Marshall LawPractical guide

Combos from Wavu Wiki (current page) · gameplay notes from TekkenDocs Season 2 Law Guide by Landon D
Game plan: close-range pressure and rhythm control. Use jabs, d/f+1, Dragon Hammer and low kicks to make the opponent press a button, then cash in with d/f+2, counter hits and the Dragon Charge branches.

Dragon Charge & Heat

Key pointsNotes
Auto-parries punchesEntering Dragon Charge during Heat auto-parries punches; a successful parry and b+2+4 both return Heat.
Heat nunchakuHEAT1234HEAT 1+2: more range, better tracking and safer across the board; on hit it launches and returns Heat.
The slide extends HeatSlide on hit → 1+2; it connects from most positions and stretches the Heat timer at the same time.
Dragon Charge controlAgainst a button, use Dragon Roar; against a step or a block, switch to the crouching slide or delay and keep pressuring.

Practical tips

TipsNotes
Get in firstLaw's strength is all at close range; from far out, only approach with safe tools like f,F+2, u/f+2 and f+3,1.
Do not finish strings on autopilotb+1,2,2 and the punch strings can be delayed, stopped, or taken into Dragon Charge; finishing them the same way every time gets ducked or punished.
Set the slide upMake them block standing with WS 3, WS 4 and the Dragon Charge mids first, then start mixing the slide in.
Save your HeatAgainst some characters, do not burn Heat early: the Dragon Charge auto-parry and the boosted 1+2 are what decide those rounds.
